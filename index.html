Questions

What people usually ask.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We build it before you pay anything. If you don't want it, we delete it and you owe nothing.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. Your domain stays in your name. We connect the new site to it and can move it to your own hosting any time.

What do you need from me?

Your current website or Google listing, your logo if you have one, and a few photos of your work. We write the copy for you to approve.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Care and Growth Plans are month to month; cancel with 30 days' notice. The subscription option has a 12-month term.

Who owns the site after launch?

On one-time packages, you own the finished site and its content once paid in full.

How do payments work?

We send an invoice you can pay by card online. Setup fees are due at launch; monthly plans bill on the same date each month.

Policies

Terms, refunds and privacy.

Refunds

If we haven't delivered your site, you can cancel for a full refund of anything you've paid. After launch, one-time fees are non-refundable, but we fix anything that doesn't match the agreed scope at no cost.

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice; the current month is not refunded.

Privacy

We use the details you send us only to build and run your website and to contact you about it. We never sell your information.

Every email we send includes a way to opt out, and we honour it right away.
